What I like to work on

I focus on the intersection of control theory, learning-based approaches and robotics: designing and deploying locomotion and autonomy systems that work reliably on real hardware.

I work on humanoid and quadruped robots, from building simulation models from CAD and identifying actuator dynamics to training and deploying control policies on real hardware. My work also includes navigation stacks and simulation infrastructure for multi-robot scenarios.

Highlights

  • Built a wheeled quadruped’s control system from scratch: turned raw CAD into a simulation-ready URDF model, identified model parameters, developed PD and RL controllers, and tested and deployed them on the real robot.
  • Unitree humanoid and quadruped locomotion controllers (RL and MPC).
  • End-to-end Nav2 autonomy stack with LiDAR-based mapping and navigation.
  • Simulation setups in Isaac Sim for complex construction-site scenarios.
  • Hands-on work with robot hardware, sensors and real-world deployment.
